## The return

Wimind was a trade guild for a long time. We counted silver, held the market, made quiet deals. But times change, and so do we.

Today Wimind returns — not as a trade alliance, but as a martial guild with a clear philosophy: **think before you strike**.

## What that means

- No toxicity, no ego.
- Discipline and respect for your word.
- A cool head in battle.

We are not chasing numbers. We care about the quality of people.

> Cogitamus ante ictum.
