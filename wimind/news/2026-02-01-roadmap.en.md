## Development plan

We have published the guild roadmap for the coming months.

## Stages

1. **Core** — gather the first 10–20 players.
2. **Economy** — set up farming, crafting and an internal market.
3. **Training** — first joint outings and PvP drills.
4. **Hideout** — claim our first base.
5. **Season** — enter seasonal events and ZvZ.

We do not promise instant results. We promise consistency.
